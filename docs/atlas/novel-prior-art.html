Artistic relatives: Tessa Campbell Fraser's Whales: a Deeper Dialogue (2024–25) turns sperm-whale codas into eidophone "eidographs" (Interalia; Winchester Cathedral); Mark Fischer's AguaSonic prints wavelet images of cetacean sound. Both make visual forms from sound; neither builds a vocabulary from a model's internal units.
