The pilot: Hardata II

420 held-out dolphin whistles, each inscribed with a counted number of bits, decoded back into sound and judged by a probe on the 2026 OpenWhistle dolphin encoder. The probe says whether the decoded sound still belongs to its whistle type. It does not say what a dolphin would hear.

Trained on decoded tones instead of originals, the probe recovers 0.75 from the 272-bit contour, level with a codec using ten times the bits: most of the gap was domain shift. Read the pilot report

What checking changed

A hidden author in the orca labels

Every SIO and SIMRES killer-whale box in DCLDE has zero bandwidth, and SIO supplies 54% of call-level Bigg's labels. Pooled box statistics separate populations by labelling convention.

DCLDE audit

Already published, so dropped

Predicting one sperm whale's next coda from its partner's appeared in Bioacoustics in June 2026 (Assayag et al.).

Excluded ideas

Already shown, so narrowed

Orca classifiers degrading on another hydrophone (Palmer et al. 2026) and calls calibrated to aerial counts (Garcia & Tolkova 2025) reshaped P3 and P5.

Novelty assessment

Announced, not available

DolphinGemma's page still says “in development”, and no weights were found. No proposal depends on it.
