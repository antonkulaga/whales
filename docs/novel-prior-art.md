### Contested image claims

SpeakDolphin and 3D Systems printed objects "seen" by a dolphin from CymaScope
patterns in 2015 ([3DPrint.com](https://3dprint.com/109082/echolocation-3d-print-dolphins/));
MIT Technology Review explained why this does not show what dolphins perceive
([critique](https://www.technologyreview.com/2015/12/10/164592/what-does-the-world-look-like-to-a-dolphin-we-still-dont-know/)).
P6 simulates echoes from known geometry instead; see also the
[earlier review](cetacean-art-precedents.md#the-cymascope-image-claim).
