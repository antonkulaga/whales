Whale and dolphin art experiments · idea 1

Follow the Phrase

Can generated music follow one particular humpback, dolphin or sperm-whale phrase, and can we show that it does? Each recording is measured into events and contours and played back as a guide in a human register. ACE-Step 1.5 then writes an accompaniment track in the context of nothing, the raw recording, the guide, or a perturbed guide, all under one caption and seed. Every output is scored against its own guide, the perturbed twin and the other recordings' guides.

recording→measured events + contours→guide in a declared register→music→alignment vs swapped + perturbed guides

Swap test:

Timing z of every output against every guide of the same species. An output that follows its phrase scores highest in its own outlined cell. A row that is equally high everywhere reacts to music in general, not to this phrase.

z = (r − mean of shifted r) / sd of shifted routlined: the output's own reference

What the scores mean

  • Timing z. Correlation between the output's spectral-flux onset envelope and bumps at the guide's onsets (σ ), against the same output circularly shifted by at least .
  • Events F1. Detected output onsets matched to guide onsets within ±; "chance" is the mean F1 after shifting.
  • Pitch z. Pitch-class agreement inside tonal events at the single best transposition, against shifted outputs. Tonal species only.
  • Rank. Where the output's own guide falls when all same-species guides and perturbed twins are ranked by timing z.
  • Source leak. Peak waveform correlation with the conditioning audio within ±100 ms. A high value means the output contains the source rather than answering it.

What was held fixed

What this does not show

  • Register shifts, instruments, captions and response rules are our artistic choices, not properties of the animals.
  • Dolphin whistles and sperm-whale clicks are detected from the waveform, so detections can include noise. Humpback units come from an annotator's selection table.
  • The sperm-whale sequences join separate DSWP clips; the gaps between clips are ours.
  • A listener enjoying the mix says nothing about whale preference or meaning.
